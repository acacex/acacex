I build small robots and sensor systems and use AI tools to solve problems along the way. My two main projects so far: a robot that finds plants with a 2D LiDAR and feeds them, and an IoT sensor that tracks the temperature and humidity of a compost pile.

Applied Computer Science student at King Mongkut's University of Technology Thonburi (KMUTT), Bangkok.

## About

I got into programming in primary school and I've been competing in coding and robotics events since. My projects rarely work on the first try, and most of what I know came from fixing them. I like the part where a sketch becomes something that moves, senses or decides.

## What I build

- **Robotics:** mobile robots, sensor wiring, Arduino control code
- **IoT:** sensors that send readings to a phone
- **AI-assisted programming:** solving problems in Python with AI tools under time limits

## Featured projects

### [GrowMate](https://github.com/[username]/growmate): a fertilizing robot for closed growing areas
- **Problem:** Feeding plants one by one by hand is slow, repetitive and uneven.
- **Solution:** A tracked robot uses a 2D LiDAR to find plants beside it, stops, circles the plant and drops fertilizer pellets 30–50 cm from the trunk.
- **Tech:** Arduino, 2D LiDAR, DC motors, release valve
- **Evidence:** Built for Coding Thailand 2025 (Robotics ROS topic, regional round, 26 Sep 2025). Photos and demo video are coming to the repo.

### [LeafLink](https://github.com/[username]/leaflink): an IoT sensor for compost piles
- **Problem:** Checking compost quality means walking to the pile and inspecting it.
- **Solution:** Temperature and humidity sensors inside the pile send readings to a phone.
- **Tech:** IoT, temperature and humidity sensors
- **Evidence:** Project write-up in the repo; photos and demo video are coming.
