## Featured projects

### AI Concrete — route & production planner

<a href="https://github.com/acacex/ai-concrete-route-optimization"><img src="assets/card-aiconcrete.svg" width="100%" alt="AI Concrete — 1st Runner-Up, AI Hackathon Route Optimization 2026"/></a>

**Problem:** A ready-mix concrete company must decide, for every truck trip, which plant loads it, which truck takes it and when mixing starts. Concrete must be poured within 120 minutes, and the staff plan missed customer SLAs on all 4 test days.<br/>
**Solution:** A Python planner that first fixes wrong plant-capacity data, then builds the plan trip by trip, checking every choice against the official simulator, and searches for better options such as asking flexible customers to shift their start time.<br/>
**Evidence:** 1st Runner-Up, AI Hackathon Route Optimization 2026 (AI for Green Logistics). Official evaluator: 4/4 days pass SLA (staff plan 0/4), 96/96 orders on time and continuous, contribution +27% (1.34 → 1.70 M THB), 8% less distance, 0 wasted concrete loads.
